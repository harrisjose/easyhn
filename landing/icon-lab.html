Sheet-ghost

A cloth thrown over something that's already there — which is what easyhn is. The brief: bell taper (not parallel sides), an irregular shallow hem (not even scallops), and small flat eyes. Those three things are the difference between a draped sheet and a Pac-Man ghost.
